Privacy on this website.

This notice covers the public company-information site at MacroDiving.com.

Last updated 29 October 2026.

This siteHostingEmail contactOther websites

A public information site.

This version introduces Macro Diving Inc. and the platform we are developing. It has no member registration, media-upload form, checkout, contact form, advertising tracker or site analytics script. It does not intentionally set browser cookies or store information in your browser.

All photographs, fonts and styles used by these pages are served with the site. They are not loaded from a third-party font service, social embed or external image tracker.

Technical information needed to serve pages.

Our hosting provider is Render. Serving and securing a website involves technical request information, which may include an IP address, browser information, the requested page and request time. Hosting infrastructure can process this information even when a site has no account or analytics features.

Render’s handling of information is described in its privacy policy. You can contact Macro Diving Inc. with questions about this company website; Render’s policy describes its own data practices.

If you contact us.

If you email Macro Diving Inc., your email provider and our email provider process the message. We receive the address you send from and the information you choose to include. We use that information to read and respond to your enquiry.

Please avoid sending passwords, identity documents, payment information or other sensitive material in a general enquiry. You can use our company contact page for privacy questions or requests relating to an enquiry you sent us.

Links to other websites.

Image credits and references link to external websites. Following a link takes you to a service with its own privacy practices. This site does not embed their account, advertising or social-media features.

The community platform is a separate release.

The community and mobile apps are in development. Before offering their account and media features publicly, we will need privacy information that describes those services and how they handle profiles, uploads, messages, location and account records.

This notice may be updated when this public site or its services change.
