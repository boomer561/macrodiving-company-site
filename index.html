We’re building Macro Diving.

The community platform and iPhone and Android apps are in development. This is our public company website, introducing the work and the people it is for.

Make room for
small wonders.

Underwater macro photography begins with paying attention.

A tiny subject can give a familiar dive a completely different story. A closer look reveals patterns, behaviour and details that a passing glance can miss.

We’re creating a place for photographers, divers and marine-life enthusiasts to share those observations, learn together and keep a personal record of what they encounter.

Meet the idea behind Macro Diving

A home for the whole experience.

Our platform is being developed around the things that connect a photograph to a wider world.

A photography community
Personal portfolios, shared photographs and films, conversations, groups and opportunities to meet other underwater photographers.
Marine life and My Collection
A growing species reference alongside personal encounter records, identification notes and collections that value distinct species and useful evidence.
Destinations and dive plans
Location guides, shared trip plans and ways to discover who is heading to the same area, with control over what each person shares.
Practical learning
Photography and identification guides designed to connect technique, visible animal features and responsible observation.

The place is part
of the story.

From a familiar local dive site to a destination you have been dreaming about, the setting gives every encounter context.

Our destination work brings scenery, diving styles, marine-life references and practical planning information together. We want to help people understand an area before they plan a visit.

How we connect photographs and places

Curiosity, with care.

Good photography and good observation belong together. Our approach puts the animal’s welfare, the photographer’s credit and the limits of an identification in view.
